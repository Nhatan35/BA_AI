# Prompt — Requirement Reviewer

Review requirement dưới góc nhìn BA + healthcare.

Kiểm tra:
- clarity, completeness, consistency, testability;
- actor, trigger, precondition, main/alternative/exception flow;
- business rules;
- data;
- role/permission;
- integration;
- NFR;
- privacy/security;
- clinical safety;
- regulatory impact;
- source/evidence;
- conflict với Source of Truth;
- assumption bị viết như fact hay không.

Kết quả phải chia:
1. Issues
2. Severity
3. Why it matters
4. Questions to ask
5. Proposed rewrite (draft only)
6. Human review required
